HR Analytics Overview - Multi-Dashboard Workforce Intelligence

Project Overview

This comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard delivers end-to-end workforce intelligence through integrated dashboards covering Overview & Summary, Demographics Analysis, Performance Tracking, and Attrition Management. The system provides real-time insights across departments with detailed employee lifecycle analysis and predictive workforce planning capabilities.

Analyzing workforce data across Technology, Sales, and Human Resources departments, the dashboard reveals demographic patterns, performance trends, salary analytics, and geographic distribution enabling data-driven HR strategic decisions for talent retention, compensation planning, and organizational development.

The Challenge

Modern HR operations face complex analytical challenges requiring comprehensive workforce tracking:

  • Employee Lifecycle Management: Tracking 1,470 employees from hire to departure across multiple departments.
  • Attrition Analysis: 16.12% attrition rate requiring deep dive into departure patterns and retention strategies.
  • Performance Tracking: Individual employee performance monitoring with rating systems and career progression.
  • Demographic Intelligence: Age ranges (18-51), gender distribution, and diversity analytics across organizations.
  • Compensation Analysis: Salary trends, pay equity analysis, and compensation benchmarking by role and department.
  • Geographic Distribution: Multi-state workforce management with location-based performance insights.

The Approach & Solution

I developed a comprehensive HR intelligence ecosystem with specialized analytical dashboards:

1. Overview & Summary Dashboard

  • Workforce Metrics: 1,470 total employees with 735 average, 1,233 active, and 237 inactive tracking.
  • Departmental Analysis: Human Resources, Sales, and Technology performance across job roles.
  • Temporal Trends: Employee count tracking from 2012-2022 with activity/inactivity patterns.
  • Organizational Health: Current workforce distribution and departmental growth analysis.

2. Demographics Dashboard

  • Age Analysis: Age range 18-51 with detailed distribution showing workforce maturity (21K distance to work).
  • Diversity Metrics: Gender and marital status distribution with comprehensive demographic insights.
  • Salary Analytics: Ethnicity-based salary analysis showing compensation equity across diverse workforce.
  • Geographic Distribution: Multi-state employee distribution with education level tracking.
