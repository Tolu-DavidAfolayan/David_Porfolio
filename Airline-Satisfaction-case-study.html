Project Overview

This comprehensive airline satisfaction prediction project leveraged machine learning to analyze 129,880 passenger records and identify the key drivers of customer satisfaction. Using advanced feature engineering and multiple ML algorithms, I developed a predictive model that achieved exceptional performance in classifying passenger satisfaction levels.

The analysis uncovered surprising insights about which factors truly drive airline satisfaction, revealing that digital experience and service quality significantly outweigh traditional operational metrics like flight delays. This data-driven approach enables airlines to optimize resource allocation and improve customer experience strategically.

The Challenge

Airlines face complex challenges in understanding and predicting customer satisfaction:

  • Multi-dimensional Data: 25+ variables across demographics, services, and operational metrics.
  • Imbalanced Classes: 56.6% neutral/dissatisfied vs 43.4% satisfied passengers.
  • Feature Selection: Identifying which of dozens of factors actually drive satisfaction.
  • Prediction Accuracy: Need for reliable real-time satisfaction prediction.
  • Business Insights: Translating complex ML results into actionable business strategies.
  • Resource Optimization: Understanding where to invest for maximum satisfaction impact.

The Approach & Solution

I implemented a comprehensive machine learning pipeline using industry best practices:

1. Machine Learning Pipeline

  • Data Preprocessing: Handled missing values, encoded categorical variables, feature scaling.
  • Feature Engineering: Created derived features and encoded 25+ variables optimally.
  • Model Selection: Compared Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and Logistic Regression.
  • Hyperparameter Tuning: Optimized model parameters for best performance.

2. Advanced Analytics

  • Feature Importance Analysis: Identified top satisfaction drivers using tree-based methods.
  • Correlation Analysis: Mapped relationships between service ratings and satisfaction.
  • Demographic Segmentation: Analyzed satisfaction patterns across customer segments.
  • Performance Validation: Comprehensive model evaluation with confusion matrices.
Key Discovery

Digital experience matters more than operational excellence! Online boarding and WiFi service are the top predictors of satisfaction, while flight delays have minimal impact on customer sentiment.

Strategic Insights Discovered

  • Digital Experience Rules: Online boarding and WiFi combine for 30% of prediction power.
  • Service Over Operations: Service ratings matter 10x more than flight delay metrics.
  • Business vs Leisure: Travel type significantly impacts satisfaction expectations.
  • Class Differentiation: Premium class experience drives higher satisfaction rates.
  • Age Factor: Demographic variables have surprisingly low predictive power (1.88%).
  • Technology Integration: Tech-savvy passengers have different satisfaction drivers.

Business Impact & Recommendations

Digital Infrastructure Priority

Invest heavily in online boarding systems and in-flight WiFi - these are the strongest satisfaction drivers.

Service Quality Focus

Prioritize service training over delay reduction - service scores predict satisfaction better than punctuality.

Targeted Interventions

Use model predictions to identify at-risk passengers and proactively address their concerns.

Resource Allocation

Reallocate budget from delay mitigation to digital experience and premium service improvements.

Counterintuitive Findings

Surprising Discovery

Flight delays have minimal impact on satisfaction! Departure delays (0.45%), arrival delays (0.58%), and total delays (0.57%) rank among the least important features. This challenges conventional airline thinking and suggests focusing on service quality over punctuality optimization.
