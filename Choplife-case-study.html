ChopLife Nigeria - Multi-Dashboard Performance Overview

Project Overview

This comprehensive ChopLife food delivery analytics project delivers end-to-end business intelligence through specialized dashboards covering Revenue Momentum, Operational Efficiency, Investment Profile, and Market Exposure. The system provides real-time insights across Nigeria's major cities with detailed performance tracking and customer behavior analysis.

Analyzing operations across 10 major Nigerian cities including Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, and Port Harcourt, the dashboards reveal geographic performance patterns, subscription tier analysis, and marketing effectiveness enabling data-driven strategic decisions for market expansion and operational optimization.

The Challenge

Food delivery operations in Nigeria face unique analytical challenges requiring comprehensive tracking:

  • Multi-City Operations: Performance tracking across diverse Nigerian markets with varying demand patterns.
  • Subscription Management: Three-tier system (Basic, Premium, Standard) with different service levels.
  • Delivery Optimization: 85.19% success rate with average delivery time of 54.54 minutes.
  • Marketing ROI: ₦781M marketing spend with conversion tracking across multiple channels.
  • Geographic Coverage: 27.78% geo coverage across Nigeria with 488.70 average deliveries per state.
  • Customer Acquisition: Multi-channel approach via Google Ads, Instagram, referrals, and social media.

The Approach & Solution

I developed a comprehensive food delivery intelligence ecosystem with specialized analytical views:

1. Revenue Momentum Dashboard

  • Revenue Tracking: ₦70.01M peak performance with detailed cost analysis (₦28.03M).
  • Meal Performance: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner revenue comparison across subscription tiers.
  • Customer Segmentation: Age group analysis (45-54, 35-44, 25-34, 18-24) showing demographic preferences.
  • Referral Intelligence: Google Ads, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Friend referrals tracking.

2. Operational Efficiency Dashboard

  • Delivery Performance: 85.19% success rate with 54.54 minutes average delivery time.
  • Order Status Tracking: Real-time monitoring of delivered (85.19%), failed (5.18%), and in-transit orders.
  • Payment Methods: Transfer, Wallet, and Card payment distribution analysis.
  • Delivery Partners: ChopLife Fleet, Gig, and Kwik partner performance comparison.

Lagos leads with ₦6.42M revenue! Port Harcourt follows with ₹7.21M, while Lagos maintains 459 orders showing strong market penetration across Nigeria's economic centers.
