Telco Customer Retention Analytics - Multi-Dashboard Intelligence

Project Overview

This comprehensive Telco Customer Retention analytics project delivers end-to-end telecommunications intelligence through specialized dashboards spanning Customer Profile Analytics, Churn Analysis, Risk Forecasting, and Insight Summary. The integrated system provides real-time insights across customer demographics, service preferences, and retention risks.

Analyzing telecommunications operations with 7,032 total customers and tracking 1,869 churned customers (26.6%), the platform reveals critical patterns in customer behavior, service utilization (Fiber optic 69.4%, DSL 24.56%), and payment preferences enabling strategic retention initiatives and revenue protection across the $16.06M yearly revenue base.

The Challenge

Telecommunications companies face complex customer retention challenges requiring comprehensive analysis:

  • High Churn Rate: 26.6% customer churn rate (1,869 of 7,032 customers) requiring immediate retention strategies.
  • Risk Prediction: 2,252 risky customers identified needing proactive intervention to prevent churn.
  • Service Optimization: Multiple internet service types (Fiber optic, DSL, No service) with varying retention rates.
  • Payment Method Impact: Electronic check (57%), Bank transfer (14%), Credit card (12%) affecting customer loyalty.
  • Contract Analysis: Month-to-month (89%), One-year (9%), Two-year (3%) contract preferences impacting retention.
  • Demographic Segmentation: Senior citizens (25%), Partner status (48%), Dependents (30%) requiring targeted approaches.

The Approach & Solution

I developed a comprehensive telco customer intelligence ecosystem with specialized retention analytics:

1. Customer Profile Analytics

  • Demographic Insights: Male (930) vs Female (939) customer distribution with detailed age and dependency analysis.
  • Subscription Patterns: 55% customers with >1 year tenure, showing loyalty trends and retention opportunities.
  • Service Adoption: Device Protection (29% vs 65%), Online Backup (28% vs 66%), Phone Service (91% vs 64%).
  • Payment Preferences: Paperless billing adoption (74.91% vs 25.09%) indicating digital engagement levels.

2. Churn Analysis Dashboard

  • Churn Metrics: 7,032 total customers with 1,869 churned, $455.66K monthly charges, $16.06M yearly revenue.
  • Gender Impact: Female (3.48K) vs Male (3.55K) churn patterns with demographic-specific retention needs.
  • Contract Analysis: Month-to-month contracts showing 55.11% higher churn vs long-term commitments.
  • Service Dependencies: Internet service types (Fiber optic 44.03%, DSL 34.36%, No service 21.62%) affecting retention.
Fiber Optic Service Leadership

Fiber optic services dominate with 69.4% adoption! Despite higher revenue potential, fiber customers show different churn patterns compared to DSL (24.56%) users, indicating need for service-specific retention strategies and premium service value communication.
