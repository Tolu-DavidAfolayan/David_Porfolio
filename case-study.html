Project Overview

This comprehensive BMW Vehicle Sales Analytics project delivers end-to-end automotive business intelligence through interactive dashboards spanning Revenue Performance, Model Analysis, Geographic Markets, and Sales Channels. The multi-dashboard system provides real-time insights across global automotive markets with detailed model, country, and channel analysis.

Analyzing BMW operations from 2019-2023, the dashboards reveal performance patterns across model lines including Z4 (666 units), 8 Series (641 units), M4 (620 units), and i8 (615 units), enabling data-driven strategic decisions for inventory management, market penetration, and revenue optimization.

The Challenge

BMW's global automotive operations face complex analytical challenges requiring comprehensive tracking:

  • Multi-Model Portfolio: Performance tracking across diverse vehicle lines from sports cars to luxury sedans and electric vehicles.
  • Geographic Complexity: Revenue optimization across diverse global markets including USA, Canada, South Africa, Kenya, and Mexico.
  • Channel Distribution: Sales performance across Online (3K units, 22%), Wholesale (7K units, 45%), and Dealership (5K units, 33%).
  • Premium Pricing Strategy: Managing high-value products from $77.4K average to premium models reaching $78.2K.
  • Market Penetration: Country-specific performance with variance rates tracking growth opportunities.
  • Seasonal Trends: Multi-year revenue tracking showing growth patterns and market cycles.
